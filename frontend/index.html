Method note. The model is trained on US BTS records joined to historical hourly weather at the departure airport. For a request it uses Open-Meteo's hourly forecast for the scheduled hour when the date is within its window, otherwise current conditions as a proxy. Forecast error, air-traffic restrictions, aircraft rotation and crew issues are not modelled.
